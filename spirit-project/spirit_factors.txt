Spirit Evidence and Factors for AI Context
13 Perception Change Factors
1.	Non-disabled people working closely with disabled people.
2.	Focusing on similarities instead of differences.
3.	Providing new knowledge about disability, including ableism.
4.	Getting to know the person behind the disability.
5.	Time and opportunity for reflexivity.
6.	Moments of realization and awareness.
7.	Engaging with provocative art that is emotionally striking and cognitively challenging.
8.	Witnessing a disabled person demonstrating their capabilities.
9.	Co-production and inclusion of disabled people in leadership roles.
10.	Challenging self-limiting beliefs in disabled people.
11.	Disability awareness and inclusivity training.
12.	Employment of disabled people.
13.	Positive media representation.

5 Modifiable Perceptions
1.	Non-Binary Thinking: Recognizing that 'ability' exists on a spectrum, challenging binary categorisations of disabled/non-disabled.
2.	Inclusivity Awareness: Sensitivity to inclusive language, provisions, and practices.
3.	Insight and Confidence: Gaining confidence in discussing and engaging with disability topics.
4.	Knowing Your Limits: Acknowledging gaps in understanding and the need for further education.
5.	Capabilities: Valuing unique skills and abilities of disabled individuals, challenging assumptions of incompetence.

Combined Framework
The 13 factors and 5 modifiable perceptions work synergistically to initiate, deepen, and sustain perception change:
Interpersonal Engagement
•	Factors: 1, 2, 4, 8, 9
•	Perceptions: 1, 3, 5
•	Focus: Building connections through shared experiences and demonstrating capabilities, fostering a nuanced understanding of ability as a spectrum.
Knowledge and Reflexivity
•	Factors: 3, 5, 6, 10, 11
•	Perceptions: 2, 4
•	Focus: Providing opportunities to learn, reflect, and challenge preconceived notions, reinforcing inclusivity awareness and personal growth.
Cultural Representation and Advocacy
•	Factors: 7, 12, 13
•	Perceptions: 2, 5
•	Focus: Leveraging media, art, and employment to represent disability positively, driving societal shifts toward equity and empowerment.
Empowerment and Inclusion
•	Factors: 9, 10, 12
•	Perceptions: 1, 5
•	Focus: Encouraging disabled individuals to take on leadership roles and challenge self-limiting beliefs, fostering inclusivity and respect for capabilities.
